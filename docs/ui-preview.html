The overlay

Bottom-centre of the screen, exactly as it appears while you hold the hotkey. Toggle the state to see the pen lift and the stroke pulse while Whisper works. Switch the stage to dark to check the translucent plate for a halo.

The settings window

One Tk window for both platforms, reachable from the tray. Change any field to see the dirty-tracking behaviour: Save and Cancel stay disabled until something actually changes, and the footer says so.
